Privacy Policy — Hop London

Last updated: 2026-07-08

Hop London is an independent, personal project that shows live London public transport data. It is not affiliated with, endorsed by, or operated by Transport for London (TfL).

What data the app uses

Location. If you allow it, the app reads your device's location to show nearby stops and sort arrivals by distance. Location is used only on your device to query the TfL API for nearby stops — it is never sent anywhere else, stored, or shared. If you don't grant location access, the app falls back to a central London view and still works fully.

Transport data. Stops, arrivals, journeys, line status, and live train positions are fetched directly from TfL's Unified API, an open, keyless public data feed, straight from your device to TfL's servers.

What the app does not do

  • No user accounts, sign-in, or profile data.
  • No backend server of its own — there's nothing in the middle to log or retain your requests.
  • No analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking SDKs.
  • No data is sold or shared with any third party.

Journey history

Planned journeys are kept only in memory (and, on the web version, in your browser's session storage) so a page refresh doesn't lose your selection. This is never transmitted anywhere and clears when you close the app or tab.
